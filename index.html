Your first visit, made simple

New to AM Dental? Here's what to expect.

  1. 1

    Request a time

    Send an appointment request online or give us a call. We'll get back to you to agree a time that suits you.

  2. 2

    Get to know us

    We'll welcome you, go through a few forms at the office and take a gentle, thorough look at your teeth and gums.

  3. 3

    Plan together

    We'll explain what we found in plain language and talk through your options, so you can decide what's right for you.

Comfort & care

Calm visits, careful hygiene

We know a dental visit isn't everyone's favourite hour. So we go at your pace, explain each step before we start and stop whenever you need a break.

  • Blankets, headphones and a quiet room if you'd like them
  • Instruments cleaned and sterilised between every patient
  • Clear explanations, and no pressure to decide on the day
