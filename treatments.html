Treatments

Comprehensive dental care for every stage of life, all in a welcoming and caring environment.

Preventive & family Restorative Cosmetic Clear aligners Emergency care

Treatment descriptions are general information, not a diagnosis. We'll recommend care only after an examination.

Preventive & family

Check-ups & cleanings

Regular exams and a professional clean to keep teeth and gums healthy.

What to expect

Who it's for: everyone, usually about twice a year (we'll suggest what suits you).

The visit: a check of your teeth, gums and mouth, X-rays when they're needed, and a gentle clean and polish by a hygienist. We'll share any findings and tips for home care.

Children's dentistry

Relaxed, friendly visits that help children feel at home in the dental chair.

What to expect

Who it's for: babies, children and teens. A first visit around the first birthday is a good start.

The visit: short, playful check-ups with a parent nearby, brushing tips and, where suitable, protective treatments such as sealants or fluoride.

Restorative

Fillings

Repair small areas of decay and help protect the tooth from further damage.

What to expect

Who it's for: teeth with a cavity or a small break.

The visit: we numb the area, remove the decay and fill the tooth, usually with a tooth-coloured material, in a single visit.

Dental crowns

A custom cap that strengthens and protects a damaged or weakened tooth.

What to expect

Who it's for: teeth with large fillings, cracks or after root canal treatment.

The visits: usually two. We shape the tooth, take a scan or impression and fit a temporary crown, then fit your permanent crown.

Dental bridges

A fixed way to replace one or more missing teeth next to healthy ones.

What to expect

Who it's for: people missing a tooth, with healthy teeth on either side.

The visits: similar to a crown. We'll talk through bridges, implants and dentures so you can compare.

Dental implants

We'll explain whether an implant suits you, and plan it with you or refer you to a specialist.

What to expect

Who it's for: adults missing one or more teeth, after a check of the bone and gums.

The process: a consultation and X-rays or a scan first. Treatment takes several visits over a few months; we'll explain each stage and its costs before you decide.

Cosmetic

Teeth whitening

Professional whitening, in the office or at home, after a check that it's right for you.

What to expect

Who it's for: adults with healthy teeth and gums who'd like a lighter shade. Whitening doesn't change fillings or crowns.

The process: a check-up first, then either an in-office session or custom trays to use at home. Some people notice short-term sensitivity.

Veneers

Thin, custom-made shells that can change the shape or shade of front teeth.

What to expect

Who it's for: adults with chipped, worn, uneven or discoloured front teeth.

The process: a consultation, then usually two or three visits. Veneers often need a thin layer of enamel removed, so we'll discuss gentler options too.

Cosmetic bonding

Tooth-coloured material to repair small chips, gaps or uneven edges.

What to expect

Who it's for: small chips, gaps and uneven edges.

The visit: usually one visit, often without numbing. Bonding can stain or chip over time and may need touching up.

Clear aligners

Clear aligners

A series of removable, clear trays that gradually move teeth.

What to expect

Who it's for: teens and adults with mild to moderate crowding or spacing. Some cases need traditional braces or a specialist.

The process: a consultation and a digital scan, then a new set of trays every few weeks, worn most of the day, with regular check-ins. Treatment length varies from person to person.

Emergency care

Toothache care

Prompt care to find the cause of the pain and make you comfortable.

What to expect

What to do: call us as early in the day as you can and we'll do our best to see you promptly.

The visit: an exam and usually an X-ray to find the cause, relief for the pain and a plan for any further treatment.

Broken or chipped tooth

Care after an accident or a bite on something hard.

What to expect

What to do: keep any broken pieces, rinse gently with water and call us. If a whole adult tooth is knocked out, call us straight away.

The visit: we check the tooth and nearby teeth, then repair it with bonding, a filling or a crown, depending on the damage.

Dental emergency? Call us

For severe swelling, trouble breathing or swallowing, heavy bleeding or a serious injury, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
